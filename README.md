## 📖 About

Welcome!

This repository documents my journey through the **NeetCode 150**, one of the most respected interview preparation roadmaps for mastering **Data Structures & Algorithms**.

Rather than rushing to complete all 150 problems, my focus is on building a deep understanding of problem-solving patterns, writing clean and efficient code, and developing the consistency required to become a better software engineer.

Every accepted solution is automatically synced from NeetCode, making this repository a living record of my progress.

## 📌 Learning Philosophy

For every problem, I aim to understand:

* The brute-force approach
* Better and optimal solutions
* Time and space complexity
* Trade-offs between different approaches
* The underlying algorithmic pattern
* How similar techniques apply to future problems

The objective is not just to solve problems, but to become someone who can solve new ones with confidence.
